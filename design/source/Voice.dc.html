Voice & copy

How Argus speaks.

A watchman, not a hype man: precise, calm, and never louder than the situation.

Calm when fine, loud only when not.

Healthy states get one quiet line. Words get shorter and more direct as risk rises.

Say the number and the fix.

Every warning names the position, the health factor, and the exact action: “Add $14,200 USDC to return to 1.50x.”

Myth is seasoning, not sauce.

Mythic language belongs to brand moments: landing, loading, empty states, the Radar. Never in a critical alert.

Never promise what Argus cannot do.

No “guaranteed”, no “never get liquidated”, no “risk-free”. Argus watches and warns; markets still move.
