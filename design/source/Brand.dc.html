Contrast rules

Black text on terracotta4.07:1Headlines and 18px+ bold only. Body copy on terracotta sits on a sand or ink card.
Terracotta 500 on ink4.07:1Fills, icons and large text only. Links and small text use Terracotta 400 (5.38:1).
Primary button5.38:1Terracotta 400 fill with ink text in the product; ink fill with sand text on the terracotta landing.
Sand 200 on ink11.86:1Default text. Sand 500 (5.57:1) for secondary text; Sand 600 only for borders.
Critical vs terracotta1.65:1Close in lightness, so critical always adds a form cue: tinted row, wide red eye, never colour alone.
