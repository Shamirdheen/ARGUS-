Why the name

Named after the watchman who never slept.

Liquidations don't happen to people who are watching. They happen at 3 a.m., during a quiet market, to the one position nobody checked.

QUESTIONS

Does Argus need my keys or a signature?

No. Argus reads public on-chain positions. Watching a wallet needs only its address.

Which protocols does it read?

Kamino, Marginfi, Drift, Jupiter, Raydium, Meteora, Orca, Sanctum and Phoenix. [CONFIRM list for v2]

What happens when a position nears 1.0x?

Its eye opens wide, the row turns terracotta, the browser tab changes, and Argus shows the exact amount to add. [DECIDE: approve vs automatic]

Is this financial advice?

No. Argus shows risk and options. Markets can move faster than any alert.
