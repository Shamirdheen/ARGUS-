Concept 1 · the mark

Hundred

small version: 13 dots
One hundred dots on the golden spiral: the ratio Greek mathematicians studied, and the arrangement of eyes in a peacock's tail. One dot is terracotta: the eye that is on you. No literal eye anywhere, yet it says hundred eyes. Sketch test: a circle full of spiralling dots, one coloured.
